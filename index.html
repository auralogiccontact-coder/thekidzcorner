Our programs

A program for every age & stage.

From your baby's very first weeks through the year before kindergarten — there's a warm, welcoming spot for them here.

Infant
6 weeks – 18 months

Gentle, one-on-one care that follows your baby's own routine for eating, sleeping and play.

Toddler
18 – 35 months

Hands-on exploration and early language & social skills, built around a predictable daily rhythm.

Preschool 3's
Entering K in 2028

Letters, numbers, and creative play that build the confidence and curiosity kindergarten calls for.

Preschool 4's
Entering K in 2027

Kindergarten-readiness skills wrapped in a full, fun school day — academics, art, music & outdoor time.

Why families choose us
